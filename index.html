Hi, I am Shurui Peng (彭书睿), an undergraduate student at Sichuan University. I plan to begin my Ph.D. at Tsinghua University in 2027, working on embodied intelligence with Yunxin Liu and Ting Cao.

My research interests lie in embodied intelligence, robot learning, and visual perception. I also work on image restoration, including all-in-one restoration through spherical expert routing. I am happy to connect with researchers working on related topics.
