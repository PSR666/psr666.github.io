Shurui Peng 彭书睿

Hi, I am Shurui Peng (彭书睿), an undergraduate student at Sichuan University. My research interests include embodied AI, robot learning, and image restoration.

I am interested in visual perception and learning-based agents for complex environments. My recent work explores all-in-one image restoration through spherical expert routing. Feel free to drop me an email if you are interested in these topics!
